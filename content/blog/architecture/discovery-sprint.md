Complex software projects rarely fail because of a lack of effort. They fail because the team never reached a shared understanding of the problem. A discovery sprint exists to reduce that risk.

## The goal

Create enough shared clarity to decide what to build next, and what not to build at all.

## A practical 5?step sprint

1. **Problem framing** ? Capture the business outcomes, not the solution.
2. **Stakeholder map** ? Identify who is impacted and what success looks like.
3. **Process walkthrough** ? Map the current workflow, step by step.
4. **Constraints review** ? Surface compliance, data, integration, and timeline realities.
5. **Lean plan** ? Agree on a phase?one build that delivers value quickly.

## What you get

- A clear problem statement
- A short list of must?have features
- A build plan and timeline assumptions
- Risks you can actually see early

When complexity is high, clarity is a deliverable. Treat it like one.
