Maintenance is rarely glamorous, but it is the difference between a system that quietly supports your team and one that constantly drains attention.

## What maintenance actually covers

- Monitoring and alerting
- Bug fixes and security patches
- Dependency updates
- Performance tuning
- Small UX improvements

## The risk of skipping it

Without maintenance, small issues pile up. Work becomes reactive, release cycles slow down, and risk grows quietly in the background.

## A simple budgeting rule

Allocate a small, consistent percentage of your original build cost each quarter. The compounding benefit is stability, predictability, and lower total cost over time.

Reliable systems are maintained, not just built.
